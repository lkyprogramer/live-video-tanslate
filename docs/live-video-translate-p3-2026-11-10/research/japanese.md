## Bottom line

The strongest genuinely missed public route is **streaming-ja-asr-anon/parakeet-streaming-ja-fully-open / M1-6.nemo**: a 123M Japanese-native cache-aware hybrid RNNT/CTC, not the previously tested 0.6B Japanese TDT Parakeet. It merits a small feasibility gate, not a claim of superiority. Check NeMo/CPU or cache-enabled ONNX export before integrating: no M1 Pro benchmark or drop-in Mac runtime has been verified. The paper compares ReazonSpeech NeMo-v2 (620M), NOT the user's Reazon k2/Zipformer baseline. Its CC-BY-NC weights restrict commercial deployment.

A second distinct route is **phonetic recognition → disambiguation/translation**: 20.6M Zipformer, 24.2M DistilHuBERT, 95.7M WavLM, and 315.6M wav2vec2 hiragana models. They lower model size but give up kanji; kana error rate is not written-text CER, and the downstream translator must resolve homophones. Thus low occupancy opportunity, not demonstrated improved subtitles. Existing Hy translation quality from kana is unverified.

## Quality and exclusions

Primary ISCA paper + released495MB .nemo + author code is strongest reproducibility chain; original streaming metric is throughput on largeGPU, not single-streamMac latency. HF communitycards are direct builder evidence but self-reported, no independent baseline-aligned product evaluation. Hiragana cards' “CTC cannot hallucinate” claim is too strong: non-autoregressive CTC still can insert/substitute erroneous content; do not repeat zero-hallucination claim. Their KER is not comparable to written JapaneseCER.

No additional public/checkpoint-verified NII/NTT/LINE/PFN/Sakana light Japanese route emerged from these queries; this is coverage evidence, not proof none exist. NTT2025SSM streaming paper surfaced but no released Japanese checkpoint verified. prj-beatrice/japanese-hubert-base-phoneme-ctc-v4 is a94.4M pronunciation/phoneme front-end, not turnkey written ASR/Chinese subtitle route, so retained as supporting lead only.

sherpa onlineZipformerCTC index fetched: explicit small25MB variant found is Chinese-only, must not infer Japanese from runtime multilingual support. hayamimi is a routed pipeline largely reusing existingReazon/sherpa, not a novel acoustic model; no claim it fixes failedbaseline. Cohere/Qwen/Moonshineofficial/Haas/Parakeet0.6B and Reazonbaseline are already covered; not relabeled new omissions.

Self-assessment: real breakthrough in **coverage** (one clear new compact native Japanese cache-streaming model; severalphonetic low-size alternatives; privateacademic route), no demonstrated breakthrough in userproductquality, M1latency or residentmemory.
